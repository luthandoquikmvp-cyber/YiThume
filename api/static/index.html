How YiThume works

1. Build your basket

Shop products from every seller and market on the network, in one cart.

2. Confirm with a code

We send a one-time code to your phone so we know the order is really yours.

3. A runner collects

A local runner picks up from the seller or market stall — no warehouse needed.

4. Delivered to your door

Give the runner your delivery PIN when your goods arrive. Pay cash or EFT.

Track your order

Enter your order code (e.g. YT-A1B2C) or the phone number you ordered with.
